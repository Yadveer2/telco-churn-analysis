# Telecom Customer Churn Analysis

## Business Problem
A telecom company is losing a large share of its customers each period.
This project identifies who is churning, why, how much revenue is lost,
and what retention actions the company should take.

## Questions I'm answering
1. What is the overall churn rate and how much revenue is lost?
2. Which customer segments churn the most?
3. What factors drive churn?
4. Can we build a simple risk score to flag at-risk customers?
5. What should the business do, and what is it worth?

## Tools
SQL, Python (Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib), Excel, Power BI
